# Geopolitica brand

- `geopolitica-logo.png`: the full logo (gold globe with connected cities over the wordmark, on midnight navy), 1448×1086. The original.
- `geopolitica-globe.png`: the globe alone, cropped from the logo, for icons and avatars.

Derived copies used by the apps (regenerate from these if the logo changes):
- `apps/*/public/brand/logo.jpg`: web-sized full logo (the Atlas welcome page)
- `apps/*/public/brand/globe-96.png`, `globe-256.png`: rounded globe marks (headers)
- `apps/*/src/app/icon.png`, `apple-icon.png`, `favicon.ico`: browser and home-screen icons

Colours sampled from the logo: midnight navy `#08101c`, gold `#ae8248`, glow ivory `#fef6e1`.
